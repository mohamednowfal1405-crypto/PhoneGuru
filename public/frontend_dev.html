01 · About the role

Shape how millions discover their next phone

At PhoneGuru, the Frontend Developer is the bridge between our product vision and our users. You'll be responsible for building and maintaining the visual, interactive layer of our platform — from the homepage and phone listing pages to our real-time comparison tool and AI chatbot interface. Every pixel you ship is seen by millions of phone buyers across India and beyond.

This role is ideal for a developer with 1–3 years of hands-on experience who is comfortable writing clean, responsive HTML/CSS/JS and has worked with at least one modern JavaScript framework. You don't need to know everything — but you should be curious, detail-oriented, and care deeply about the end user's experience.

02 · What you'll do

Responsibilities

  • Build responsive, cross-browser UI components for PhoneGuru's phone listing, specs, and comparison pages using React and Tailwind CSS.
  • Translate Figma designs into clean, semantic HTML and CSS that look great on mobile, tablet, and desktop.
  • Integrate frontend components with REST APIs to display live phone specs, prices, and availability data.
  • Maintain and improve the performance of existing pages — fixing layout bugs, reducing load times, and improving Lighthouse scores.
  • Collaborate with the backend team to connect new product features to the UI.
  • Write unit and integration tests to ensure component reliability across updates.
  • Participate in sprint planning, code reviews, and daily standups as part of an agile team.
03 · Requirements

What we're looking for

  • Minimum 1 year of professional frontend development experience (internships count if the work was substantive).
  • Solid understanding of HTML5, CSS3, and vanilla JavaScript — you know the fundamentals before reaching for a framework.
  • Hands-on experience with React.js — you've built at least one real project or feature using components, props, and state.
  • Familiarity with responsive design principles and CSS frameworks like Tailwind CSS or Bootstrap.
  • Basic understanding of how REST APIs work and experience fetching and displaying data on the frontend.
  • Comfortable using Git for version control — branching, merging, and raising pull requests.
  • A portfolio, GitHub profile, or any live project you're proud to show us.
04 · Nice to have

Bonus points

  • Experience with TypeScript — even basic type annotations go a long way.
  • Familiarity with Next.js or any SSR/SSG framework.
  • Prior work on an e-commerce, product listing, or comparison website.
  • Basic knowledge of web performance metrics like LCP, FID, and CLS.
  • Any personal project related to tech, gadgets, or mobile phones — we'll be very impressed!

06 · Life at PhoneGuru

What we offer

  • Salary between ₹6 LPA and ₹12 LPA depending on your experience and skills, reviewed every 12 months.
  • Hybrid work schedule — 3 days in our Bengaluru office, 2 days from home.
  • ₹20,000 annual learning budget for courses, books, and certifications — we'll pay for that Udemy course you've been eyeing.
  • MacBook or Windows laptop of your choice, fully set up before your first day.
  • Health insurance covering you and your immediate family.
  • Direct access to our product team — your ideas and feedback actually ship.
  • Free access to every phone we review — and yes, sometimes you get to keep one.
