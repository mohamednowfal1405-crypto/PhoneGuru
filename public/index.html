About PhoneGuru

PhoneGuru is your go-to buddy for finding the perfect smartphone without the confusion. We make comparing phones super easy — just pick two models and see how they stack up on battery life, display, processor speed, camera quality, storage, and overall performance. Not sure what to buy? Built with Firebase for fast updates and a smooth experience, PhoneGuru is here to make picking your next phone simple, fun, and stress-free.
