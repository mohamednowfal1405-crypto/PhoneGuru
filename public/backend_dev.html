01 · About the role

Power the engine behind millions of phone decisions

At PhoneGuru, the Backend Developer is the foundation everything else is built on. You'll design and maintain the APIs, databases, and server-side systems that drive our phone specs database, real-time price tracker, comparison engine, and AI chatbot — serving millions of requests every day across India and beyond.

This role is perfect for a developer with 2–5 years of experience who is comfortable designing RESTful APIs, working with relational and non-relational databases, and writing server-side code that is fast, reliable, and easy to maintain. You'll work closely with our frontend, data, and DevOps teams to ship features end-to-end and keep our platform running at peak performance.

02 · What you'll do

Responsibilities

  • Design, build, and maintain RESTful APIs that power PhoneGuru's phone specs pages, comparison tool, price tracker, and chatbot interface.
  • Own the data pipeline that ingests, cleans, and stores phone specification data from manufacturers, retail partners, and third-party sources.
  • Optimise database queries and schema design across PostgreSQL and MongoDB to keep response times fast as our data grows.
  • Collaborate with the frontend team to define and document API contracts, ensuring smooth integration between client and server.
  • Implement authentication, authorisation, and rate-limiting systems to keep user accounts and data secure.
  • Write automated tests (unit, integration, and end-to-end) and participate in code reviews to maintain high code quality.
  • Monitor production services, investigate incidents, and drive root-cause analysis and post-mortems when things go wrong.
  • Contribute to infrastructure decisions — containerisation, deployment pipelines, and cloud architecture.
03 · Requirements

What we're looking for

  • Minimum 2 years of professional backend development experience; production experience on a user-facing product is strongly preferred.
  • Proficiency in at least one backend language — Node.js (Express / Fastify), Python (Django / FastAPI), or Java (Spring Boot).
  • Solid experience designing and consuming RESTful APIs; familiarity with API documentation tools like Swagger or Postman.
  • Hands-on experience with relational databases (PostgreSQL or MySQL) — writing queries, designing schemas, and managing migrations.
  • Working knowledge of NoSQL databases (MongoDB or Redis) and when to use them over relational alternatives.
  • Comfortable with Git, pull requests, and code review workflows in a team environment.
  • Basic familiarity with Linux/Unix environments and deploying applications to cloud platforms (AWS, GCP, or Azure).
04 · Nice to have

Bonus points

  • Experience building or consuming webhooks, message queues (RabbitMQ, Kafka), or event-driven architectures.
  • Familiarity with Docker and container orchestration tools like Kubernetes.
  • Prior work on a data-heavy product — spec databases, product catalogues, price comparison engines, or search systems.
  • Experience integrating third-party LLM APIs (OpenAI, Anthropic, Gemini) into backend workflows or chatbot pipelines.
  • Knowledge of caching strategies using Redis or CDN layers to reduce server load at scale.
  • A side project, open-source contribution, or GitHub profile that shows us what you love to build.

06 · Life at PhoneGuru

What we offer

  • Competitive salary between ₹10 LPA and ₹20 LPA based on your experience, skills, and interview performance — reviewed every 12 months.
  • Hybrid work schedule — 3 days in our Bengaluru office, 2 days working from wherever you're most productive.
  • ₹30,000 annual learning budget for courses, certifications, books, and conferences — AWS certifications, system design courses, whatever helps you grow.
