01 · About the role

Write the words that help India buy smarter

At PhoneGuru, great content is how millions of buyers make up their minds. A first-time smartphone buyer in Patna, a power user choosing between the Galaxy S25 Ultra and the iPhone 16 Pro, a parent looking for a safe phone under ₹10,000 for their kid — they all land on PhoneGuru looking for clarity. You'll be the person who gives it to them.

As a Mobile Technology Content Writer, you'll write phone reviews, in-depth spec comparisons, buying guides, and SEO articles that are accurate, engaging, and genuinely useful. You don't need to be a developer — but you do need to love phones, understand tech deeply enough to explain it simply, and care about writing that is clean, opinionated, and trustworthy. This role is remote-friendly: we care about the quality of your words, not where you type them.

02 · What you'll do

Responsibilities

  • Write detailed, well-researched phone reviews covering camera performance, battery life, display quality, software experience, build quality, and value for money.
  • Produce spec comparison articles and head-to-head pieces (e.g. "Redmi Note 14 Pro vs Poco X7 Pro") that help users make confident decisions quickly.
  • Write SEO-optimised buying guides targeting high-intent search queries like "best phone under ₹20,000 in India 2025" — working with our SEO team on keyword targeting and structure.
  • Cover mobile industry news — new launches, price drops, software updates, and manufacturer announcements — with speed and accuracy.
  • Maintain and update existing articles to keep specs, prices, and recommendations accurate as the market changes.
  • Collaborate with the data team to incorporate price trend data and user behaviour insights into content.
  • Contribute to PhoneGuru's editorial voice guidelines and help onboard future content contributors.
03 · Requirements

What we're looking for

  • Minimum 1 year of experience writing about technology — phones, gadgets, consumer electronics, or any adjacent beat. Published work we can read is essential.
  • Deep knowledge of the Android and iOS ecosystems — chipsets, camera hardware, display tech, battery and charging standards, and the Indian smartphone market segments.
  • Strong writing skills in English — clear, concise, and confident. You have a voice without being inaccessible.
  • Comfortable with SEO fundamentals — keyword intent, heading structure, meta descriptions, and internal linking. You write for readers first, search engines second.
  • Ability to work independently, manage multiple articles simultaneously, and meet deadlines without hand-holding.
  • Genuinely curious about technology and passionate enough to keep up with new launches, leaks, and trends without being told to.
04 · Nice to have

Bonus points

  • Prior bylines at a recognised tech publication — GSM Arena, 91mobiles, The Mobile Indian, Gadgets360, AnandTech, or similar.
  • Hands-on phone reviewing experience — you've benchmarked devices, tested cameras in real conditions, and written detailed verdicts.
  • Comfort writing in both long-form (2,000+ word reviews) and short-form (300-word news updates) formats.
  • Basic understanding of how spec databases work and familiarity with reading official teardown data or chipset spec sheets.
  • A personal tech blog, YouTube channel, or social following in the mobile space — extra points if it's already helping people buy better phones.
  • Working knowledge of Tamil, Hindi, Telugu, or Kannada — multilingual content is on our roadmap.

06 · Life at PhoneGuru

What we offer

  • Salary between ₹5 LPA and ₹10 LPA based on your experience, portfolio quality, and writing assessment — reviewed every 12 months.
  • Remote-friendly setup — work from anywhere in India. We do team meetups in Bengaluru once a quarter.
  • ₹15,000 annual learning budget for writing courses, SEO tools, journalism workshops, or any resource that makes you a better writer.
  • A laptop allowance of ₹40,000 to set yourself up with the hardware you need to work comfortably.
  • Comprehensive health insurance covering you and your immediate family.
  • Every phone we receive for review passes through your hands first — full hands-on time before you write, and yes, some of them you keep.
  • Your byline on a platform that millions of real buyers read and trust every month.
